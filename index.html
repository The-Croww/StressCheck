about

StressCheck.

StressCheck is a psychology-inspired self-assessment tool designed to help you understand your current stress levels. Our quiz uses validated psychological principles to provide meaningful insights into your emotional and physical well-being. We believe that self-awareness is the first step toward better mental health. By providing a simple, accessible tool for stress assessment, we aim to empower individuals to recognize their stress patterns and take proactive steps toward wellness.

Our assessment evaluates both physical and emotional indicators of stress through a series of carefully crafted questions. You'll respond using emoji reactions that reflect your current state, making the process intuitive and engaging.
